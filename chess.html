'O'odham English

Gidahimel (War Expedition)

The pieces’ roles are briefly explained below followed by a brief comparison to traditional O’odham roles in warfare.

Cicvikuḍ Cu:cu’idag (Game Pieces)

♔ Je:ñim-Cekcim (Smoke Keeper)
This piece is able to move one square ahead, behind or to the side, it can also move one square diagonally, this goal of the game is to potect this piece. If the king is checkmated, the player loses the game
This peice is similar to the one called smoke keeper, whose job it was to hold meetings with the warriors and instruct them about war whenever it was time to fight.
♕ Ha-'u'adam (War Leader)
This piece can move as many pieces in any direction, ahead, behind, to the side or diagonally.
This piece is similar to the one called War Leader in English, or literally "The Carrier" in O’odham, and who would direct the fighters in strategy and movement.
♗ Ma:kai (Doctor)
This piece can only move diagonally but may move as many squares as possible.
This piece is similar to the role of the doctors that accompanied the war parties and whose job it was to advise and determine strategy of the enemy party, they would advise on aspects such as position or expected time of attack.
♘ Ceggiadam (Warrior)
This piece can move two squares either ahead, behind or to the side, it then turns and moves one square, or it can move just one square ahead, behind or to the side, turn and then move two more squares. This piece can jump over other pieces and long on the other side of them..
This piece closely resembles those who engaged in direct combat in war and were skilled with the shield and club.
♖ 'Amogdam (Announcer)
This piece can move any number of squares ahead, behind or to the side.
This pieces is similar to the one who carries messages to the piece, and whose job it was to travel around and announce what has been decided.
♙ Gidaihimdam (Scout)
This piece is only able to move one square ahead, or two squares if it is the first time that the piece moves. If it goes to the other end of the board and reaches the other player’s first rank, it can become a war-leader, a doctor, a fighter or an announcer.
This is similar to those whose job it was to go and scout the enemy’s location and activities in order to plan a better attack.
